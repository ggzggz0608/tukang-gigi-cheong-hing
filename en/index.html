Four Generations of Craft

Tukang Gigi Cheong Hing was established in 1950 and is now carried on by the fourth generation, Hung Lam In. For more than 70 years we have kept the craft handed down through our family — making and fitting dentures traditionally, by hand.

Every piece is made carefully, drawing on the experience, skill and technique inherited from one generation to the next.

Our mission is to keep walking towards a hundred-year-old business, while preserving the art and traditional craft of denture-making so that this heritage can continue to be passed on.
